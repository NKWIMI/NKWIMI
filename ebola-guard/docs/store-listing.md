# Store listing (draft)

**App name:** Ebola Guard – Symptom Check & Info
**Short description (80 chars):** Ebola symptom & exposure check, prevention info, and an AI health assistant.

**Full description:**

Ebola Guard helps you understand Ebola and know what to do if you are worried.

• Symptom & exposure check — answer a few questions based on WHO and CDC public-health guidance and get clear next steps. Works offline.
• AI health assistant — ask questions about Ebola symptoms, how it spreads, prevention and safe care, in your own language.
• Learn — how Ebola spreads and does not spread, warning signs, prevention, and common myths.
• One-tap emergency contacts — call your health hotline quickly.

If you have a fever or other symptoms and may have been exposed to Ebola, separate yourself from others and call your health hotline or emergency number immediately.

IMPORTANT: This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Only a laboratory test can confirm Ebola. AI answers may contain mistakes; always follow the advice of health workers and your health authorities.
