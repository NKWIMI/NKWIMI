## What the app collects
- **Screening answers** stay on your device. They are only sent to our server if you choose "Ask the AI assistant about this", as a short summary.
- **Chat messages** you type are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate an answer. We do not ask for your name, and you should not enter identifying details.
- **Reports** of AI answers you flag (the answer text and the reason).
- **Optional account.** If you create one, we keep your **first name and e-mail** (and a secure, scrambled version of your password) with our account provider, Supabase [region]. Your **profile picture stays on your phone** and is never uploaded. Your screening answers are **never** added to your account. Without an online account, the profile is only saved on your phone.
- We do **not** collect your location, contacts, or advertising IDs, and we do not show ads.

## How we use it
Only to provide answers in the app, prevent abuse (temporary IP-based rate limiting), and improve answer safety from reports. We never sell data or use it for advertising.
